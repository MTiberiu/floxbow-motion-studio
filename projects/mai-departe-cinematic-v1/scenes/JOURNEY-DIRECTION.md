# Three paths — continuous journey prototype

User direction replaces the actor-led cut: fixed central spheres, future grid on the right, revealed past on the left. Scene advances left continuously, film-strip edge perforations follow transport. Whole-song prototype uses existing sampled Phrase Emotional, Vocal Prosody and Word Gravity from motion-guides.json; it does not execute the external HTML or claim bit-identical reconstruction of its interactive settings. HTML inspected for camera and signal scaling.

2D vector experiment: shaded spheres, seeded spray, soft reveal mask, layered mountains, sunlight, flowing water, fish and birds. Future skill content intentionally blank. No Three.js or raster textures yet. Reveal currently combines a soft past mask and spray halos; no persistent paint simulation. All behavior is deterministic from frame, including pause/seek.

Previous character Timeline preserved in white-stage-timeline-backup.json. Do not run prepare-film.mjs unless deliberately restoring actor direction. Lyrics and all non-director clips unchanged. No renders.
